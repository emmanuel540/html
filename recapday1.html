Kenyan Tourism

Welcome to Kenya

Kenya is one of the most popular tourism destinations in Africa. It is famous for its wildlife, beautiful landscapes, beaches, mountains and rich culture.

Visitors from around the world come to Kenya to experience nature, adventure and African culture.

Why Visit Kenya?

Kenya offers many exciting experiences for tourism. Some popular reasons to visit Kenya include:

  • Beautiful wildlife
  • National parks and game reservers
  • Beautiful beaches
  • Mountains and scenic landscapes
  • Rich African culture

Popular Tourist Destinations

Kenya has many places that tourists can visit. Some of the most popular destinations are:

  1. Maasai Mara National Park
  2. Amboseli National Park
  3. Tsavo National Park
  4. Mount Kenya
  5. Diani Beach
  6. Lake Nakuru

Things Tourists Can Do

Tourists can enjoy many different activities while visiting Kenya

  • Go on a safari
  • Visit the beach
  • Climb Mount Kenya
  • Watch wildlife
  • Visit cultural villages

Popular Kenyan Wildlife

Kenya is home to many different animals. Some of the famous animals that tourists come to see include:

  1. Lions
  2. Elephants
  3. Giraffes
  4. Zebras
  5. Cheetahs
  6. Rhinos

Tourism Terms

Safari
A journey where tourists travel through natural areas to observe wildlife.
National Park
A protected area where wildlife, plants and natural environments are preserved.
Game Reserver
An area ste aside to protect wild animals and their habitats.
Tourist
A person who travels to another place for pleasure, exploration or learning.

Kenyan Culture

Kenya has many different communities with unique traditions, languages, foods, music and dances.

Tourists can learn about Kenyan culture by visiting local communitiesc, cultural centres and traditional villages.
